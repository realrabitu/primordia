What is Primordia?
Primordia is a 2D platformer game based on the Geological Time Scale (GTS) of the Earth. Users may choose from different geological eons, eras, and periods and get to play as lifeforms of their chosen period. The goal is to survive and meet the levels' objectives. 

Author's notes (5 July 2026)
It is designed as a senior high school capstone project, so the features are expected to be incomplete and buggy. It is important to point out that the game is made with the assistance of AI tools (vibe-coded) and its mechanics are implemented with AI. However, it is also necessary to distinguish the work of the AI from the work of the people behind this project. Almost all of the GUI elements, graphics, and visuals are entirely made by hand by our designers. As you may also notice, the repository looks horrible since, at the time, I did not know what I was doing with Git and GitHub. Despite that, I truly believe it was a fun project and served as a learning experience. I do have the final, working, and "finished" version of the game, but the repository will remain as it is: a remnant of what was once a vibe-coded senior high school capstone project. I also plan to remake this project in the future, with enough time, care, effort, and renewed knowledge and skills.
