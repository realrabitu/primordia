# About the Project

**Primordia is a biology-inspired 2D platformer game based on the Geological Time Scale (GTS) of the Earth.**  It blends scientifically accurate concepts and immersive design to provide an engaging and meaningful learning experience about evolution and geological periods.
Users may choose from different geological eons, eras, and periods and get to play as lifeforms of their chosen period. The goal is to survive and meet the levels' objectives. 

## Features
* **Paleozoic and Mesozoic Era-inspired terrain and organisms**
* **PC-optimized controls and gameplay**
* **Play as or interact with ancient marine and land organisms**
* **Original sprites and art**
